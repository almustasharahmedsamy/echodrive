# EchoDrive production roadmap

1. Foundation: command loop, localization, settings, Windows speech bridge. **Complete**
2. Spatial audio core: listener, azimuth, distance attenuation and stereo pan. **Complete in 0.2**
3. Content catalog: 15 authored streets and 25 vehicle profiles. **Complete in 0.2**
4. Native audio backend: streamed OGG samples, engine loop and 15 ambience variants. **Complete in 0.3**
5. Traffic simulation: nearby vehicles, distance alerts and automatic-driving response. **Complete in 0.4**
6. Weather and environments: rain, storm, wind, heat, humidity, cold, snow, ice, desert, mountain and farm. **Complete in 0.4**
7. Desktop UI: accessible menus, keyboard remapping, high contrast and screen-reader labels.
8. 3D runtime: renderer, vehicle physics, collision and camera systems.
9. QA: keyboard-only and blind-play testing, performance, installer and signing.
