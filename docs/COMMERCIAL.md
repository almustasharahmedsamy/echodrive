# Commercial and IP policy

EchoDrive source code, game design, brand, original documentation and original integration work are proprietary and all rights reserved. The public GitHub repository is source-visible for review and collaboration requests only; public visibility does not grant reuse, redistribution or commercial rights. Third-party audio remains under its own licenses and must be checked before any paid distribution.

Possible commercial models are: a paid Windows edition, a free accessible demo with a paid full edition, institutional accessibility licenses, and later optional content packs. Do not advertise a final consumer price until a playable graphical build, native-language QA, installer, support plan and store fees have been costed.

The current repository is a prototype foundation, not a finished AAA game. A realistic commercial budget must be split into programming, 3D art, sound recording and mixing, localization, accessibility testing, QA, legal review, installer/store work and marketing. The market report delivered with the task should be used as a planning estimate rather than a promise of revenue.
